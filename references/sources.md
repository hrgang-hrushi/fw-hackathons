# Source trail

Checked 2026-10-05. Official event pages and Devpost Help Center are authoritative for their own events and platform workflow; individual project pages are team-authored accounts. For a new hackathon, re-open its current rules and challenge pages.

## Dataset and gallery audit

- [Devpost Hacks, 2,222-submission dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/twangodev/devpost-hacks): nine event configurations, winner labels, descriptions, technology tags, demo links, and embedded GitHub README excerpts. The dataset card limits reuse to a research rationale and notes mixed upstream rights.
- [HackWinnerDB repository](https://github.com/notsointresting/hackwinnerdb): structured winner index and source URLs. See the [data audit](data-audit.md) for verification-status counts; the data license is CC BY 4.0.
- [Large Devpost projects dataset](https://huggingface.co/datasets/alvanlii/devpost-hackathon-projects): historical project-event corpus and event metadata, last updated January 2025 per its card. Its prize field is not a clean winner label.
- [TreeHacks 2025 gallery](https://treehacks-2025.devpost.com/project-gallery), [Cal Hacks 12.0 gallery](https://cal-hacks-12-0.devpost.com/project-gallery), [HackGT 12 gallery](https://hackgt-12.devpost.com/project-gallery), [PennApps XXV gallery](https://pennapps-xxv.devpost.com/project-gallery): public event-level checks against dataset records.
- [Devpost organizer project-data export](https://help.devpost.com/article/86-metrics-export-projects-data): official CSV workflow for organizers with management access.

## Event rules and criteria

- [HackTX 2025 overview](https://hacktx2025.devpost.com/): build window, in-person judging, listed judging criteria, sponsor challenges including ElevenLabs and Gemini, and event-specific MLH digital video language.
- [HackUTD 2025 rules](https://hackutd-2025.devpost.com/rules): registration and team requirements, build window, expo judging, two-sponsor-challenge limit with MLH exception.
- [LA Hacks 2025 overview](https://la-hacks-2025.devpost.com/): examples of MLH GoDaddy Registry, Gemini, Auth0, Cloudflare, and MongoDB Atlas prizes.
- [TreeHacks 2025 overview](https://treehacks-2025.devpost.com/): grand prize criteria and sponsor challenge descriptions including ElevenLabs.
- [HackPrinceton Fall 2025 overview](https://hackprinceton-fall-2025.devpost.com/): creativity, utility, charity, avidity criteria.
- [Gemini 3 Hackathon winners update](https://gemini3.devpost.com/updates): announced podium including Globot.

## Submission process

- [Devpost: Tips for Planning Your Project](https://help.devpost.com/article/125-tips-for-planning-your-project): draft early and plan submission work; its one-week recommendation is for a longer example event.
- [Devpost: Know Your Submission Steps](https://help.devpost.com/article/126-know-your-submission-steps): typical project fields, video, links, and final review; actual forms vary.
- [Devpost: Uploading a Demo Video](https://help.devpost.com/article/85-uploading-a-demo-video): accepted hosting and visibility guidance, and distinction between project and submission video.
- [Devpost: How to Edit a Submission](https://help.devpost.com/article/123-how-to-edit-a-submission): changes to a submission are limited by its deadline.
