# Winner patterns: observed, not guaranteed

Research checked 2026-10-05. These are selected public Devpost examples. The [four-source audit](data-audit.md) covers all 2,222 records in a released comparison table; the cases below are deeper reads, not a representative random sample. Award labels and event criteria come from Devpost; implementation details are self-reported by teams. The takeaways are **inferences** about what to test in a new event, not proof that any feature caused a win.

| Event / award | Public evidence | Practical inference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [LA Hacks 2025: Alto, 1st overall and Fetch.ai track](https://devpost.com/software/alto) | Team described a specific mobile bug workflow from intake through analysis, replication, and notification, and named distinct agent roles. | A narrow end-to-end task can make technical complexity legible. |
| [LA Hacks 2025: Lumos, 2nd overall and AI Agents League](https://devpost.com/software/lumos-f8h0mn) | Team described a multimodal, location-aware experience and discussed privacy and integration challenges. | Make the cross-service workflow and user benefit visible, and explain tradeoffs. |
| [LA Hacks 2025: OpenSesame.Work, 3rd overall](https://devpost.com/software/opensesame-work) | Team discussed reliability of retries and parallel processing plus a simple interface for backend automation. | A clear user interface can carry a complex workflow; reliability matters to the demo. |
| [HackUTD 2025: SpendingWithSpendy, Best Design and Capital One](https://devpost.com/software/spendingwithspendy) | Child-focused financial literacy flow used a mascot, card interaction, parent view, and screenshots; team acknowledged NFC setup difficulty. | Interaction design and coherent branding can align with a sponsor problem, but the risky hardware path needs a fallback. |

Additional overall-placement cases from the full-table review show substantial variety: [FaceTimeOS, Cal Hacks 12.0 first overall](https://devpost.com/software/facetime-macos-ai-agent) used a voice-controlled desktop flow; [Dose, HackGT 12 first overall](https://devpost.com/software/dose-ebmo9z) paired a sensor-equipped bottle with a dashboard; [SoundShield, PennApps XXV first overall](https://devpost.com/software/soundsheild) used a wearable sound response; [Baymax, TreeHacks 2024 grand prize](https://devpost.com/software/baymax-your-personal-healthcare-companion) centered a robotic assistance scenario; and [HawkWatch, TreeHacks 2025 grand prize](https://devpost.com/software/hawkwatch) centered a surveillance workflow. These examples weaken any claim that one UI framework, algorithm, or agent pattern is necessary. Each project's performance claims still need independent verification.

## Cross-case synthesis

In these examples, winning pages usually articulate **a specific user situation, a visible result, a technical mechanism, and obstacles encountered**. Several combine a recognizable interaction with a deeper system behind it. The set does **not** establish a preferred framework, algorithm, visual style, or number of agents. The event rubric is the stronger decision input.

Published rubrics also vary: [HackTX 2025](https://hacktx2025.devpost.com/) listed creativity, implementation, presentation, and learning, and described its Best Overall prize through design, complexity, and impact. [HackPrinceton Fall 2025](https://hackprinceton-fall-2025.devpost.com/) listed creativity, utility, charity, and avidity. [TreeHacks 2025](https://treehacks-2025.devpost.com/) emphasized innovation, functionality, and overall excellence for its grand prize. Therefore copy neither a winner's stack nor this package's sample score weights blindly.

## What this research cannot show

Devpost pages are portfolios and may include aspirational features or post-event updates. Awards establish that the entry won the named category, not that every described feature was working during judging. Selection is biased toward visible, well-documented entries. There is no evidence here for a universal winning formula or a guaranteed top-three outcome.
