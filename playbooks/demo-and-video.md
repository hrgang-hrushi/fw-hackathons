# Live demo and video

Read the event's time, format, and video rules first. HackTX 2025 emphasized showing the built project rather than relying on a pitch; its page describes special two-minute rules for fully digital MLH events. Do not apply that length or wording to every event. See [sources](../references/sources.md).

## Demo arc

1. State the user and pain in one sentence.
2. Show the starting input or situation.
3. Perform the key action live or in an honest recording.
4. Show the computed result and why it changes a decision.
5. Point to one technical differentiator and, if needed, sponsor evidence.
6. Close with measured outcome and one honest limitation.

For a two-minute limit, an example budget is 10s hook, 20s problem/input, 65s product flow, 15s technical proof, 10s result/close. Adjust to the actual event. A screen recording with readable zoom and clear audio usually carries more evidence than a cinematic montage. Use captions or on-screen labels when possible. Keep the cursor deliberate and remove private tabs/notifications.

## Script template

```text
"[Project] helps [specific user] do [job]. Today they [pain].
Here is [representative input]. I [perform action].
The system [visible transformation], and the result is [outcome].
The key technical choice is [why it matters]; [sponsor service] provides [specific role].
On [test/sample], we observed [measured result]. [Known limitation] remains."
```

Rehearse with someone who has not seen the project. Test audio, resolution, captions, links, and video visibility in a private/incognito window. Keep a local backup and screenshots for a live-demo failure. Devpost's video help says the submission's video can differ from the portfolio project video; verify the exact video attached to the submission before cutoff.
