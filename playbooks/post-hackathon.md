# Post-hackathon follow-up

Within 48 hours, preserve the judged submission and video, thank collaborators and mentors where appropriate, document what was truly built, and triage bugs and exposed secrets. If feedback is available, map it to rubric criteria and specific product improvements. Share a short project writeup with demo, source, role of each teammate, learning, and limitations; avoid unsupported prize or impact claims.

Within two weeks, decide whether to maintain, archive, or grow the project. If continuing, interview real users, prioritize one retention or task-success metric, replace demo shortcuts, improve accessibility and security, and open contributor-friendly issues. A clear maintained repo is more useful to GitHub visitors than a large abandoned feature list.
